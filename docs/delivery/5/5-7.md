## Requirements

### Primary Requirements
1. **Pre-Incubation Document Types**: Support for multiple document types including:
   - Pre-Incubation Agreements
   - Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Agreements
   - Non-Circumvention Agreements
   - Assessment Registration Forms
   - Legal Contract Templates

2. **Document Structure**: Based on Ditsogo Group example, include:
   - Agreement metadata (parties, duration, registration numbers)
   - Confidentiality terms and definitions
   - Non-circumvention clauses
   - Signature requirements and verification
   - Legal compliance tracking

3. **M&E Integration**: Documents must integrate with existing MOV system:
   - Link to participant records
   - Status tracking (draft, signed, verified, archived)
   - Compliance monitoring and reporting
   - Document version control

4. **Document Management**: Full lifecycle management:
   - Template creation and customization
   - Document generation from participant data
   - Digital signature workflow
   - Document storage and retrieval
   - Audit trail and compliance reporting

### Technical Requirements
1. **Data Model Extension**: Extend MOV types to support legal documents
2. **Document Templates**: Create template system for agreement generation
3. **Signature Workflow**: Implement digital signature collection and verification
4. **Storage Integration**: Secure document storage with Firebase Storage
5. **Compliance Tracking**: Monitor agreement status and expiration dates
6. **Legal Document UI**: Specialized interface for legal document management

## Notes

### Legal Document Requirements (based on Ditsogo Group example):
1. **Agreement Parties**: Clear identification of incubator and incubatee
2. **Duration Terms**: Specific start and end dates (typically 3 months)
3. **Assessment Process**: Framework for evaluating potential incubation
4. **Confidentiality Clauses**: Protection of business information
5. **Non-Circumvention**: Prevention of business opportunity circumvention
6. **Signature Requirements**: Authorized representatives from both parties
7. **Domicilium**: Legal addresses for both parties
8. **Governing Law**: South African legal framework compliance

### Integration Points:
1. **Participant Onboarding**: Auto-generate agreements during registration
2. **MOV System**: Link legal documents to intervention verification
3. **Compliance Dashboard**: M&E visibility into legal document status
4. **Notification System**: Alerts for signature requirements and expiration
5. **Audit Trail**: Complete history for legal and compliance purposes

### Security Considerations:
1. **Document Encryption**: All stored documents must be encrypted
2. **Access Controls**: Role-based access to legal documents
3. **Signature Verification**: Cryptographic signature validation
4. **Audit Logging**: Complete audit trail for legal compliance
5. **Data Privacy**: POPIA compliance for personal information
