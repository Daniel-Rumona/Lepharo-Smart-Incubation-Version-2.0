# Lepharo Smart Inc — "What's new" demo (v7)

Audience: Lepharo HODs, coordinators, Center Coordinators, the director, and SMEs.
Voice: Alice (ElevenLabs), each section voiced in one take. Spoken as "Leparo", "H. O. Dees", "Em-Oh-Vees", "K. P. Eyes", "Tooso", "Q. X. Core".

## Opening

Welcome to the new Lepharo Smart Incubation platform. There's a lot that's new for HODs, coordinators, Center Coordinators and the CEO: a redesigned intervention model, the Interventions Tower and digital twins, live KPIs, AI built into everyday work, notifications everywhere, and a fresh look and feel that even works offline. And for our SMEs, it's simpler than ever: sign in with just a phone number, sign documents online, get help on WhatsApp, and keep their books in Qx Core.

## 01 · A redesigned intervention model

Let's start with interventions. An HOD now designs each intervention once, in a guided wizard, with Thuso, our AI assistant, helping along the way. First, the outcome: what the business should end up with, and what should change. Thuso suggests a few options, and the system can check back later to see whether the change really lasted. Next comes the schedule. An intervention can be once-off, ad hoc, or recurring, say once a month for six months, so ongoing support no longer has to be captured over and over again. Once it's set up, the HOD assigns it to a coordinator, for each SME that needs it. The coordinator then sees everything allocated to them in one place: what's due, what's overdue, and what's waiting on the SME. And when the work is done, the proof of delivery, the MOVs, moves through clear stages, from the coordinator, to the HOD, and on to monitoring and evaluation for sign-off.

## 02 · The Interventions Tower and Digital Twins

To keep all of this on track, Center Coordinators and HODs have the Interventions Tower. It shows every intervention being tracked, what's stuck, who it's waiting on, and for how long. Open any item, and you can follow that intervention's journey step by step, see exactly where it stalled, and send a reminder to whoever needs to act. The Intervention Digital Twin models every open intervention. Press play to project the next eight weeks, then turn a dial, like more HOD review days, and see where the backlog moves before you commit. And the SME Digital Twin does the same for each business. It projects the SME's risk if nothing changes, and shows how simulated moves, like delivering a service this week, would bring that risk down. And Impact Analytics shows what all of this work is achieving for the businesses we support.

## 03 · KPIs and performance

KPIs now live in a single register, with each indicator's target tracked live from the work done in the system, so there are no separate spreadsheets to maintain. Staff performance sits right alongside it, for each financial year, so line managers and the CEO can see how their teams are doing at a glance.

## 04 · AI, built in

AI is now part of everyday work. Ask the Data Assistant a question in plain language, and it answers from your live data, often with a chart. Ask which SMEs need you this week, and it pulls together risk, evidence, deliveries and sessions into a set of SME profile cards. Set up a watcher to be told when something changes, or a chaser that follows up on outstanding work for you every weekday morning, until it's done. Every role also has a report it can play, a short guided walkthrough of their programme or department. And on Fridays, the assistant asks whether you're ready for the meeting. It all works in the background too, so you can ask a question, carry on with your work, and the assistant lets you know when your answer is ready.

## 05 · Notifications and WhatsApp

Notifications now reach people wherever they are: in the app, by email, and on WhatsApp. On WhatsApp, SMEs check appointments and send documents, HODs approve MOVs, coordinators see who needs them this week and record sessions, Center Coordinators get their reports, and the CEO keeps track of performance across the organisation.

## 06 · Phone sign-in for SMEs

Many of our SMEs don't use email, and now they don't need to. They enter their phone number, get a six-digit code on WhatsApp that expires after five minutes, and they're in. Before carrying on, every user sets up their signature and signs the POPIA privacy notice, so consent is always on record.

## 07 · Qx Core: the books, kept for them

Our SMEs also get Qx Core, where their books keep themselves up to date. Every business has its own statement address, so the bank simply emails the statement there. Qx reads it straight away and sorts every transaction into a category, ready to check. Nothing is posted until the owner confirms, and no banking passwords are ever shared. The books answer plain questions, like the cash position or this month's income statement, and they're the same figures Lepharo's reporting reads. And a business moving from Sage, QuickBooks or Excel doesn't retype anything. Qx reads the file and shows every row before anything is saved. And because Qx Core stays with the business, current and graduated SMEs can keep getting support, even in their own language, like Setswana or isiZulu.

## 08 · Look, feel, mobile and offline

Finally, the platform looks and works better everywhere, with light and dark mode, colour themes, and layouts built for phones. And it keeps working without a connection. Screens you've opened show their saved data, and anything you save is queued and synced when you're back online. One platform, one view of every SME, from intervention to impact. Thank you.
