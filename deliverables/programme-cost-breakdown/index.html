4SEDFA

SEDFA is charged an average of R16,170.48 per month. The charge includes an external system update of R300.00 per SME, which for 33 SMEs is R9,900.00 (61.2% of the monthly charge). The Platform reads each SME's financial and revenue data from the external financial system (QuantNow), which supports the Finance page and the revenue-based KPIs used in programme reporting. The update covers third-party access to that system, daily synchronisation and monitoring of the connection, credential management, error handling and reconciliation of SME revenue data.

Quantilytix System Maintenance

The fee includes R6,450.00 per month for Quantilytix System Maintenance: system-level maintenance of the Quantilytix system on which the Platform runs, covering updates, security patching, monitoring and support. It is shown on its own line so that maintenance of the underlying system is distinguished from the programme's own features.

6Frozen Modules

Kriel ESD and SEDFA have recorded almost no intervention activity in the last 90 days (SEDFA none, Kriel one MOV and two active SMEs). Intervention monitoring, reminders and automation for these programmes are frozen and resume in the month in which at least 10 SMEs and 10 MOV records record activity within a 90-day window. SME records, the document vault, hosting and backups remain live, and all data is retained.

7Basis of Charges

  • Kriel ESD and SEDFA: an average monthly cost, built from the key services that run the Platform for the programme. Smaller services are grouped under monitoring, backups and domain.
  • Sibanye Technical Training: a monthly fee for the standard features and the programme-specific capabilities. The once-off development cost of R72,000.00 is spread evenly over 36 months.
  • SEDFA external system update: charged at R300.00 per SME per month.
  • Services: Google Gemini and Anthropic (AI), Google Firebase (database, authentication, storage and functions), Vercel (hosting), Brevo (email) and WhatsApp messaging. Charges are reviewed against actual usage.
  • Volumes: Sibanye Technical Training volumes (61 SMEs, three regions, five documents per SME) are planning figures and will be reviewed against actual enrolments once recorded on the Platform.
  • Amounts: all amounts are in South African Rand and are stated to the cent.
